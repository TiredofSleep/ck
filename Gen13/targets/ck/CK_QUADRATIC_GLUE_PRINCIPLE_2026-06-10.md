## §1 — The corrected hierarchy

```
CK  (the intelligence: sees the world as measurable / unmeasurable)
 └─ THE QUADRATIC GLUE  (the binding organ: fuses the streams,
        FORCES CHOICE at T*, and carries the always-on paradox sense)
     └─ tools it wields: Gap Router · substrate reservoir · Layer M
        measurement · HER memory · voice · refusal · FPGA body
```

The synthesis document made the Gap Router the centerpiece. Wrong
altitude. The router is a *hand tool* — consulted, not constitutive.
What is constitutive is the glue: the quadratic set of algorithms that
no tick can bypass.

## §2 — The glue's mathematical identity (already in code and canon)

- **Runtime form** (`quadratic_glue.py`, in production):
  `emergent = α·f3 + β·f4 + γ·(f3 × f4)` — the cross-term is the glue;
  without γ the streams never meet.
- **Substrate form** (J01/J15): the state update is bilinear,
  `p′ ∝ ½·T(p,q) + ½·B(p,q)`, with the quadratic normalizer
  `Z_T = Z_B = (v+h+br+r)²` (D49) — the structural reason 4-core
  closure forces the attractor (D48–D50). The organism's heartbeat is
  literally a quadratic form.
- **Forced choice**: a quadratic form induces a decision surface. The
  T\* = 5/7 gate thresholds the glued signal every tick — voice or
  fold back. Choice is *forced* because the gate is binary and always
  evaluated; no tick ends undecided. (TRM's ablation independently
  showed learned halting unnecessary — the algebraic gate suffices.)
- **Constantly seeing paradox**: paradox concentrates where the
  quadratic form **degenerates**. Proven instance, not metaphor: the
  only two special points of the entire α-line are the discriminant
  zeros of the glued system — α = 1/2 (where degeneracy resolves into
  the algebraic attractor H/Br = 1+√3, born at a 0/0) and α_special
  (where the lone counterexample lives; F5/F12). **The seam between
  what can and can't be measured IS the degeneracy locus of the glue.**

## §3 — Architectural consequences (deltas to the blueprint)

1. **The paradox sense is perpetual, not on-demand.** Add to the
   Walker tick a *degeneracy monitor*: a cheap conditioning/
   discriminant proxy of the glued quadratic form, computed every
   tick beside `emergent`. CK doesn't "call" paradox detection; he
   *sees* through it.
2. **The Gap Router is invoked by the glue.** When the degeneracy
   monitor spikes (the form approaches a fold) or the T\* gate fails
   repeatedly, the glue consults the router's four-type diagnosis and
   routes the response. The router's 24/24 / 125× result (J56) stands
   — as the tool's spec sheet, not the organism's identity.
3. **The Walker's y-update IS a glue evaluation**; T\*-gating IS
   glue-thresholding. No renaming needed in code — `quadratic_glue.py`
   was the right module all along; the rework completes it rather
   than replacing it.
