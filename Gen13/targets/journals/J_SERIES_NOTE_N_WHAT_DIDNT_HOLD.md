# What didn't hold: the substrate's numerical bridges and dead ends

**2026-06-14.** A research program is judged partly by what it refuses to keep. This
note collects, in one place, every place the ℤ/10 substrate was claimed to "rhyme"
with a fundamental constant or famous problem — and states plainly that **none of
them is a derivation.** It also records the two genuine null results, which are
real findings worth keeping. Consolidating these out of the headline corpus is not
hiding them; it is being honest about their status. Each was a real attempt; each is
reported as it actually stands.

The rule, applied throughout: **a numerical coincidence is not a theorem.** A short
algebraic object flexible enough to be fit to α, to Yukawa ratios, *and* to the
Riemann zeros is flexible enough to be fit to noise. Where the underlying *algebra*
is real (e.g. a finite Gauss sum), the algebra is kept and moved to the math spine;
only the *physical/number-theoretic claim* is retired here.
