## Why the table program was retired

The program here — "TIG", Trinity Infinity Geometry in its earlier form — was built on three 10 × 10
composition tables (TSML, BHML and CL) over ten named operators, VOID … RESET.

- **Where the tables came from.** An audit found that they are AI renderings of the author's verbal
  descriptions of the ten operators. They disagree with one another on 49–71 of their 100 cells.
- **What survived.** No result specific to the tables passed three gates:
  1. true;
  2. specific — beats random tables;
  3. not a readout of how the tables were built.

  Each result was either generic, a restatement of the construction, numerology, or computed on a
  transcription error. (The "prime-11 wobble" is two swapped digits.)
- **The evidence.** In the flagship's archive: `04_meta/FOUNDATION_NULL_MODEL_AUDIT.md` and
  `04_meta/DRIFT_CENSUS.md`, at the tag
  [`archive-2026-09-24`](https://github.com/TiredofSleep/trinity-infinity-geometry/tree/archive-2026-09-24).
  The census covers all 56 J-papers. The flagship's
  [`GRAVEYARD.md`](https://github.com/TiredofSleep/trinity-infinity-geometry/blob/main/GRAVEYARD.md)
  lists every dead end, with what killed it.
- **The CK side.** A June 2026 probe found that the Coherence Keeper's from-scratch language model
  organizes its internals by **English grammar**, not by the tables
  ([`Gen13/targets/ck/trinity/TIG_AS_EXPLANATION.md`](Gen13/targets/ck/trinity/TIG_AS_EXPLANATION.md)).

   [`Gen14/targets/journals/J_series/J61/J61_CLOSURES_ALL_REALIZED.md`](Gen14/targets/journals/J_series/J61/J61_CLOSURES_ALL_REALIZED.md).
